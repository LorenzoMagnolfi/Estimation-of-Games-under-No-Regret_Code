Internal source audit, 2026-10-16. No fee-adjusted result has been promoted into the paper.

The historical Swappa notebook reads the displayed listings_table. The Stata cleaner removes the dollar sign and destrings Price, without subtracting platform or payment fees. Swappa's current Ask Price FAQ distinguishes ask from listing price: a $100 ask appears as $103 to buyers before sales tax, and sellers must include shipping in their ask. The partner-fee FAQ states that the seller fee is 3% of ask, while PayPal processing is 3.49% plus $.49 of the total order. Sources accessed 2026-10-16: https://swappa.com/faq/answer/ask-price and https://swappa.com/faq/answer/partner-fee.

The indexed official announcement dated February 23, 2022 describes the transition to 3% fees on both sides and the $100/$103 example: https://swappa.com/blog/swappa-fees/. Its direct fetch returned 404, although its indexed text was available. This supports the fee structure predating the sample, but does not verify each seller's processor, negotiated rate, tax base, or the historical display-rounding rule.

Ignoring display rounding temporarily, under a common affine receipt schedule let p be transaction price before tax, b the buyer fee on ask, s the seller fee on ask, f the processing percentage, F the fixed processing charge, and tau the expected tax rate entering that charge. Then a=(1-s)/(1+b)-f*(1+tau), and net receipts are a*p-F. For remaining per-sale cost k, profit equals a*(p-c_eff)*q(p), where c_eff=(k+F)/a. Thus k=a*c_eff-F and net operating margin is a*(p-c_eff).

Fees should not be subtracted a second time from the inferred effective gross-price margin. They already enter c_eff through the behavioral profit function. Shipping, handling, and refurbishment belong in k unless separately observed and removed. Inventory opportunity value is not automatically procurement cost.

For illustration, b=s=.03, f=.0349, F=.49, tau=0 give a=.9068476. Common positive scaling cancels from the fully normalized r/C event. This permits a transparent monetary-unit transformation under the stated schedule. Heterogeneous or action-dependent receipt coefficients do not cancel from multiproduct profits. Unknown buyer locations must not silently be assigned zero sales tax as a verified payout calculation.

A cost-quality restriction on c_eff is not the same as a procurement-cost restriction. Reference prices come from another retail channel and should not be converted by Swappa's fees. Historical display rounding, the attainable seller price grid, the focal seller's processor, and the fee base remain explicit empirical checks. The stated numerical policy menu cannot silently stand in for a different platform action set.


## Integer asking-price sensitivity, October 17 UTC

Every observed price of AMS Traders, GadgetPickup, Upgrade, Certified Cell, and SaveGadget is compatible with p=ceil(1.03*a), where a is an integer asking price. Several other rounding conventions fail this check. The data therefore support this historical price-grid assumption, although the current official FAQ alone does not establish the 2023 rounding rule. Source: continuation_clock_grid_audit.json, generated by continuation_seller_sensitivity.py. No account, listing, or checkout was accessed or modified.

The new implementation distinguishes displayed price p from pre-tax transaction amount x=1.03*a. The recovery a=floor(p/1.03), with a numerical tolerance followed by an exact displayed-price check, is unique under the maintained integer-ask rule. Upgrade's average p-x is $.343. Demand depends on displayed p, while profit is (x-c)*q(p) in effective transaction-price units. Apply the common affine fee conversion above to x, not to rounded display p. The fixed and proportional fee, tax, shipping, and inventory-value qualifications remain.

ask_price_grid.py implements the grid. ask_grid_witness.py evaluates every feasible integer asking price up to a displayed cap of $350; cost-plus policies are rounded to that same grid. A continuous-display profit bound remains a valid, deliberately conservative range certificate because x<=p. Peer margins use unrounded x_j-c_j. normalized_askgrid.py uses fixed feasible price proposals within each continuous cost cell. Its normalized own-gain lower bound checks both cost endpoints, since the gain for a fixed proposed price and probability ratio is affine in cost. This avoids an invalid smoothness assumption across price-rounding jumps. The full-grid witness and the outer bound use the same certificate.
